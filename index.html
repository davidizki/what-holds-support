Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app’s main features work without signing in.

Where is my library?
It is stored in the local app container. Use Settings → Export library to keep a portable JSON backup.

Does processing require a paid AI service?
No. The built-in processor works locally. An OpenAI-compatible endpoint is optional and may point to an open-source model on your own hardware.

Is Fact-check exhaustive?
No. It is a clearly labelled preliminary evidence scan. “Unsupported” means the scan did not corroborate a claim; it does not mean the claim was proven false.
