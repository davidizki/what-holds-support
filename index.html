Source first, or stop

The app works from what the source actually says.

A title, video footer, or page controls are not treated as content. What Holds acquires a substantive source before processing, stores that exact acquisition, and lets you inspect it beside the result.

Articles and public captions

Structured article text is preferred over navigation and footers. If a public page is only an app shell, one non-persistent on-device rendering attempt can wait for its article. YouTube links use available public caption text—not the video or audio—and stop clearly when usable captions are unavailable.

Podcasts and audio

Publisher transcripts and podcast-feed transcripts come first. When a publisher exposes audio, supported system-26 devices can transcribe it on device; the temporary download is removed afterwards.

Evidence you can open

Every note separates acquisition from processing, shows the word count and method, and provides the complete acquired text plus the original link when one exists.

Intelligence without setup

The best available private route, automatically.

There is no model picker during onboarding and no cloud account to create. What Holds chooses a safe route for each capture and records the route on the saved note.

  1. Apple on-device intelligence Used for eligible captures when Apple’s on-device Foundation Model is available, ready, and supports the current language. Summary selects exact evidence passages first and locally replaces any unsupported model wording with source text.
  2. Private deterministic engine Completes the capture automatically on older systems, ineligible devices, oversized sources, or whenever model generation cannot safely finish.
  3. Purpose-built exceptions Copy preserves the source exactly. Fact-check uses the disclosed public-evidence workflow; an LLM is never treated as evidence.
Availability varies. The app itself supports iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and macOS 14 or later. Apple on-device generation additionally requires a compatible device, a supported language and region, Apple Intelligence enabled, and a ready system model on iOS/iPadOS/macOS 26 or later. The private fallback requires none of those things.

Advanced users: an explicitly enabled OpenAI-compatible endpoint can override automatic processing. The full acquired source is then sent to that endpoint under its own terms. This is optional and is not a connection to a ChatGPT subscription. Read the setup and privacy details.

Questions before buying

Straight answers.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Compatible devices get richer on-device processing automatically when Apple’s model is ready. Every supported device retains the private deterministic route.

Do I need an AI subscription or account?

No. Version 1 is designed as a one-time purchase with no What Holds account or subscription. The advanced custom-endpoint option is entirely optional and uses credentials supplied for that endpoint—not a ChatGPT login.

Is Fact-check a professional verification service?

No. It is a deliberately limited preliminary scan of English Wikipedia. “Unsupported” means that scan did not corroborate the claim; it does not prove the claim false.

Does the first version sync between devices?

No. Each device keeps its own local library. Use JSON export for a portable backup and import it deliberately on another device.
