01 / The shift

Saving is not learning.

Your bookmarks remember where something was. What Holds helps you remember why it mattered—and whether it still holds up.

Before

A river of tabs, highlights, and forgotten good intentions.

Collection feels productive in the moment. Retrieval is the real test later.

After

A living body of ideas you can explain, challenge, and use.

Every source has a purpose. Every conclusion retains its provenance. Every useful idea gets another encounter.

The compounding effect

Knowledge changes from something you own into something you can call on.

Not a second brain that thinks for you. A practice that makes your first one stronger.

02 / Built on learning science

A product system, not a productivity superstition.

What Holds is designed around robust findings from cognitive science: retrieval strengthens access, spacing makes learning more durable, and useful feedback helps correct errors. The app turns those principles into a small repeatable loop.

Effortful recall

Remembering is not only the outcome of learning. It is part of learning.

What Holds creates short-answer review cards and asks you to retrieve before revealing. Delayed tests have repeatedly produced better retention than repeated restudy—even when restudy feels more fluent.

Roediger & Karpicke, 2006 ↗

Distributed encounters

One intense encounter fades. Well-timed returns make access more durable.

Reviews return over widening intervals based on how recall felt. Research across hundreds of experiments finds that the most useful spacing depends on how long knowledge needs to last.

Cepeda et al., 2006 ↗

Correction attached

Retrieval should meet the answer, its evidence, and its limits.

After you attempt recall, What Holds reveals the source-grounded answer. For disputed claims, the verdict and correction stay together rather than letting a memorable falsehood detach from its context.

Mendes Oliveira et al., 2022 ↗

Epistemic discipline

The app can help interpret a source. It should never make the source disappear.

Every note keeps the acquired text, origin, date, processing route, and links. This is a product trust principle: a polished conclusion is useful only when you can inspect what produced it.

See the trust boundaries ↓

Learning outcomes depend on the material, the learner, the retrieval task, and timing. What Holds applies evidence-informed principles; it does not promise a universal memory multiplier.

03 / The practice

From passing information to durable capability.

One clear loop, repeated whenever a source deserves a place in your life.

  1. 01↘

    Capture with intent

    Bring in the actual article, transcript, document, audio, or text—not a title pretending to be content.

  2. 02◇

    Choose the work

    Concept, Summary, Fact-check, Analysis, or exact Copy. The treatment follows your purpose.

  3. 03✓

    Keep the proof

    See the source, acquisition method, processing route, evidence, and limitations beside the result.

  4. 04↺

    Retrieve, then reveal

    Answer from memory before opening the card. Rate recall so the next encounter earns its timing.

  5. 05↗

    Use better judgement

    Search what you have learned, follow the supporting notes, and let durable ideas compound.
