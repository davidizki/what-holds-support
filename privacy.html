What Holds

Privacy policy

Effective 11 August 2026. What Holds is designed to work without an account. The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or use personal data for advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your captured sources, knowledge notes, review cards, review history, settings, and search data are stored locally in the app container. Share extensions use the app’s private shared container only to hand content to the main app.

Network activity

What Holds connects to the network when you ask it to retrieve a source. A preliminary fact-check sends selected claim text to public evidence sources. An external model receives source content only when you explicitly enable and use the endpoint you configure.

If you enable a remote model endpoint, source content and processing instructions are sent to the endpoint you configure and are governed by that service’s terms and privacy policy. The service may retain or process the content. Use a local endpoint if you do not want model inference to leave your hardware. External model processing is disabled by default.

The developer does not receive your library, captured content, model requests, or API credentials.

Export and deletion

You can export the library as JSON, share individual notes as Markdown, delete individual items, or erase the local library in Settings. Removing the app removes its app-container data, subject to Apple platform backup behavior.

Children

What Holds is a general-audience education and productivity tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be included with an app update and reflected by a new effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion questions can be sent to developer David Izquierdo through the public What Holds support form. Do not include private captured content or credentials.
